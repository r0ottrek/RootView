# Code of conduct

RootView is built by and for people who run small field-service businesses, and the developers who help them. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we ask

- Be kind and assume good intent. Many people here are business owners, not programmers.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Welcome first-time contributors and newcomers' questions.
- Respect people's time: search the issues before opening a new one, and include the details someone would need to help.

## What isn't okay

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission. That includes customer data from a RootView site: never post it in an issue.
- Spam or promoting unrelated products.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior here bothers you, report it privately to the maintainers through [GitHub's report feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) or a private security advisory on this repository. Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block people who don't follow this code.

For security problems, see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
